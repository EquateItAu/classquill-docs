## Overview

The ClassQuill iPad and Android app keeps working when your connection drops. You can open the app, find a class or student, start a new whiteboard and teach. Everything you create is saved on the device and syncs when you are back online.

Offline mode is for tutors and organisation staff. Students and parents need a connection.

---

## Before you go offline

Open the app once while online and signed in. ClassQuill saves your classrooms, students and today's schedule so they are ready without a connection.

You can open the app while offline as long as you were signed in before you lost the connection.

---

## Teach on a whiteboard offline

1. Open **Teaching** and choose a classroom or a one-to-one student.
2. Create a new whiteboard, or open an existing one you have used before.
3. Draw, add images and teach as normal.
4. Tap **Add a question** to place a question from a downloaded subject on the board.

When you reconnect, your boards merge with the online copy. Nothing is duplicated, and strokes you erased stay erased.

### What the board status means

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Saved** | Your work is on the server. |
| **Saving…** | Your work is on its way. |
| **Saved on this device** | You are offline. Your work is safe on the device and will sync. |
| **Couldn't sync — retrying** | You are online but the last save failed. ClassQuill is trying again. |
| **Couldn't sync — your changes are kept on this device** | The server keeps refusing the save. Your work stays on the device. Contact support if it does not clear. |
| **This board is locked; your changes are kept on this device** | Someone locked the page while you were offline. Choose the option to save your changes as a new page. |

<Note>
  Answer boards that a student is working on after submitting an attempt do not save offline.
  They show **Not saved yet — waiting for a connection** and save when you reconnect.
</Note>

## Record an in-person lesson offline

You can record a lesson with no internet.

- The recording is saved on the device in 10-minute parts, so a long lesson is safe.
- Keep recording with the screen locked. The app keeps recording in the background.
- When you are online, the parts upload automatically and ClassQuill produces one transcript and summary for the whole lesson.
- Find the recording in the session's **Recordings** section. You and your organisation's admins can play it back.

Your organisation controls how long audio is kept. The default is 12 months.

---

## Download question bank subjects

Download a subject while you are online to browse its questions offline and add them to a whiteboard. Downloads start automatically on wifi in the app.

---

## Install ClassQuill on Windows

On a Windows computer, open ClassQuill in Edge or Chrome and choose **Install app** from the account menu. The installed app updates itself at a safe moment, so you never need to reinstall.

---

## What still needs internet

- Signing in for the first time
- Chat and messaging
- Taking attendance and marking payments
- Video calls
- Downloading new question bank subjects
- Student and parent access
