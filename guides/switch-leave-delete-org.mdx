---
title: "Switch, leave, or delete an organisation"
description: "Move between organisations you belong to, leave one you no longer need, or permanently delete an organisation you own."
---

Some people belong to more than one organisation on ClassQuill — for example, if you own your own tutoring business and also help out at another. This guide covers moving between them, leaving one, and permanently deleting one.

## Switching between organisations

If you belong to two or more organisations (as an owner, admin, or tutor), an **organisation switcher** appears at the top of the sidebar, just under your organisation's name.

### 1. Open the switcher

In the sidebar, click your organisation's name at the top.

### 2. Pick an organisation

Choose one from the list — each shows your role in it (owner, admin, or tutor).

### 3. You're switched

The whole app moves into that organisation — dashboard, users, calendar, billing, and settings all reflect the one you picked.

<Note>
  Your choice is remembered, so you'll land back in the same organisation the next time you sign in — until you switch again.
</Note>

The switcher only appears when you belong to more than one organisation. If you're in just one, there's nothing to switch and it stays hidden.

## Leaving an organisation

Leaving removes your access to everything shared at the organisation level. An owner or admin can re-invite you later if needed.

### If you're a tutor

1. Go to **Settings → General**.
2. Find the **Organisation** card, click **Leave**, and confirm.

### If you're an admin

1. Go to **Settings → Organisation**.
2. Scroll to the **Danger Zone** and click **Leave Organisation**, then confirm.

<Warning>
  If you own the organisation, you can't leave it directly — that would leave it without an owner. **Transfer ownership** to someone else first, or **delete** the organisation (below).
</Warning>

## Deleting an organisation

<Warning>
  Deleting is **permanent and cannot be undone**. It removes the organisation and everything belonging to it — members, classrooms, and related records. Only the **owner** can delete an organisation.
</Warning>

### 1. Open organisation settings

Go to **Settings → Organisation**. You must be signed in as the **Owner**.

### 2. Open the Danger Zone

Scroll to the bottom and click **Delete Organisation**.

### 3. Confirm by typing the name

Type the organisation's exact name into the box — this unlocks the delete button so it can't happen by accident.

### 4. Delete

Click **Delete Organisation**. You'll be returned to your home page. If you belong to other organisations, ClassQuill switches you into one of those automatically.
