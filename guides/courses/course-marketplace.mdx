---
title: "Share and install courses with the course marketplace"
description: "List your organisation's courses for other organisations, publish versions and grant licences, or install a course another organisation has licensed to you."
---

The course marketplace lets one organisation share a course it has built with other organisations. The organisation that shares is the **publisher**. An organisation it gives access to is a **licensee**.

- Licensees get a live copy of the course. When the publisher fixes a lesson, the fix reaches every class that uses it.
- Each class stays on the **version** it installed. New pages and lessons the publisher adds only appear when a teacher updates the class.
- Nothing is charged. A publisher grants a licence; there's no checkout.

<Note>
  Publishing is invite-only while the course marketplace is new. To become a publisher, contact ClassQuill support. Installing a course you've been given a licence for is open to every organisation.
</Note>

## For publishers: list a course

You need to be an org admin, and your organisation needs to be a publisher. Build the course first as an organisation course. See [Build a course](/guides/courses/building-a-course#use-your-organisations-course).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Course marketplace">
    Go to **Settings** → **Course marketplace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the listing">
    Click **List a course** and choose one of your organisation's courses. Give it a title and a short summary. Licensees see these when they install it. **Catalogue ID** is optional, for your own reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the first version">
    Click **Publish v1**. This takes a snapshot of the course's current pages and lessons, and lists the course.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Training courses can't be listed.

### Publish a new version

When you've added or rearranged pages and lessons, click **Publish v2** (or whichever number is next) on the listing. You can add a note on what changed.

- Classes already using the course stay on their version until a teacher clicks **Update**.
- Edits to the wording or files of lessons that are already in the course reach every class straight away. You don't need a new version for those.
- While a licence is active, you can't delete or move pages and lessons that classes are using. Publish a version without them first.

### List, unlist and retire

Use the **⋮** menu on a listing:

- **Unlist** takes it back to a draft. Organisations that already hold a licence keep their access.
- **Retire** stops new licences and installs. Existing licences keep working.
- **List again** brings a retired or unlisted course back.

## For publishers: grant and revoke licences

<Steps>
  <Step title="Grant a licence">
    In the **Licences** card, click **Grant licence** and choose the listing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the organisation's link name">
    Type the link name from their public page, for example `northside-tutoring`. Click **Find organisation**. ClassQuill shows the organisation's name so you can check it's the right one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Optionally set the date access ends, then click **Grant to** *organisation*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Licences** card lists every organisation you've granted access to, with its status.

- **Revoke** removes access straight away, for every class that installed the course.
- **Restore access** gives a revoked licence back.

You can see which organisations hold a licence. You can't see their classes, teachers or students.

## For teachers: install a licensed course

Once your organisation holds a licence, any teacher can add the course to their class.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the class's curriculum">
    Open the class, go to **Learn** → **Curriculum**, and scroll below the area cards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add from the course marketplace">
    In the **Course marketplace** section, click **Add from course marketplace**. Pick a course and click **Install**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The course appears in the class's curriculum with the publisher's name on its card. Students see it in their course straight away.

Courses can be installed in classes only, not in groups or 1:1s.

### Make it fit your class

Licensed courses work like your organisation's courses:

- **Hide**, **reorder** and **rename** sections, pages and lessons. Your changes only affect your class.
- **Add your own lessons** to the publisher's pages.
- The publisher's own lessons are read-only. The publisher fixes them for everyone.

### Update to a new version

The course marketplace section shows the version your class is on, such as **v2 pinned**. When the publisher publishes a newer version, an **Update to v3** button appears. Your hides, renames and the lessons you added carry over.

Student progress is kept for every lesson that's still in the new version.

### Uninstall

Click **Uninstall** next to the course. It leaves your class's curriculum. Student progress is kept, and you can install it again while your organisation holds the licence.

## What isn't available yet

- Quizzes, worksheets and SCORM packages inside a licensed course aren't shown to licensees yet.
- Seat limits and paid licences are not available yet.
