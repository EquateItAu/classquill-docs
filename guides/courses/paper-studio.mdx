The Paper studio is where you build every paper in ClassQuill: quizzes, homework, tests and exams. You add the questions, set targets, check how the paper is shaping up and print it, all on one screen. A saved paper opens in the same studio, so you can come back and edit it.

<Note>
  The studio is for teachers, tutors and organisation admins. You build and edit papers on a tablet or a computer. On a phone the studio is read-only: you can see the totals and readiness, **Preview & print**, and **Assign**.
</Note>

## Open the studio

- **A new paper:** on a course page or a tutor training section, click **+ Add → Quiz** (or **Worksheet**), then **New quiz**.
- **A saved paper:** on your organisation's subject hub, open **Assessments** and choose **Edit in studio** from the paper's menu. On a course page, the paper card's **⋮** menu has **Edit in studio** too.

## Build the paper

The studio has four tabs: **Paper**, **Details**, **Rules** and **Release**.

On **Paper**:

- Click **Add from question bank** to search the bank and add questions. Click **Create essay task** to add an essay.
- Or attach a file instead. A paper can be questions or a file.
- Reorder questions by dragging them with a mouse, or with the up and down arrows on any device.
- Change a question's marks on its row. If you use sections, pick the question's section on its row.

**Details** holds the title, kind, subject and instructions. **Rules** holds the time limit, reading time, a practice run, and what the student may open while sitting it: notes, the reference sheet, the calculator and the AI tutor. **Release** sets when the score, the correct answers and the feedback become visible, who marks, and how students may answer.

## Sections and targets

Open **Sections & insights** (on a large screen it sits beside the paper).

- Turn on **Use sections** to group the paper into parts. Each section can have a target for **Marks**, **Minutes** and **Questions**.
- Under **Paper targets**, set the **Total marks** and **Total minutes**, a **Topic mix** (pick any level of the curriculum and give it a share of the paper), and a **Difficulty mix**.
- Click **Save targets as template** to reuse a set of targets. On another paper, choose it from **Apply template…**.

Each section shows whether it is **Below target**, **On target** or **Over target**. A target counts as met within 10%.

## Check readiness

The **Insights** tab shows the paper's **Readiness** as you edit. It checks that:

- questions have been added
- the section and paper targets are met
- the topic mix and difficulty spread match your targets
- every part is filed to a topic
- no answer keys are waiting on a check

Readiness is a guide. It never stops you saving.

## Save

Click **Save to library** for a new paper, or **Save changes** for a saved one. The button only turns on once something has changed.

## Print a booklet

Click **Preview & print** and choose what to print. The booklet has:

- a cover page with your organisation's branding, a table of sections, questions and marks with a total, and lines for the student's name, class and date
- an answer box under each part, sized for the answer it expects

<Tip>
  Branding on printed papers follows the **Brand printed papers** switch in your organisation's Brand Kit.
</Tip>
